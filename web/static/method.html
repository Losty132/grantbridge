Method

How the score is made.

Data, filters, scoring, eligibility, effort estimates, AI checks and privacy, in plain language.

01The flow

From a federal notice to a decision.

01
Profile

Your strategic plan

Each objective from the agency's OSBM-published plan is stored separately with its section and link.

02
Survey

Six quick questions

Focus areas, capacity, minimum award, cost share and prep time tune the ranking.

03
Ingest

Grants.gov, every morning

Open and forecasted notices from the public API, de-duplicated, with first-seen and last-changed dates. If the API fails, cached data is labeled as cached.

04
Filter

Hard rules first

Expired, state not eligible, match you can't provide, or award below your minimum: removed, with the reason shown.

05
Score

100-point priority score

Strategic alignment 50 (meaning + the objective's own keywords), applicant fit 20, deadline feasibility 15 (graded), resource feasibility 15. Missing information gets partial credit, never full, and is named.

06
Decide

People decide

Pursue, Maybe or Pass (a reason is required to pass). Nothing is ever submitted automatically.

02Eligibility

Rules and citations, never AI alone.

Four labels

Appears Eligible · Needs Human Verification · Partnership Opportunity · Not Eligible for Direct Application. They come from the Grants.gov applicant codes plus sentences quoted from the notice.

Conditions, quoted

Matching funds, partners, letters of support, pre-applications and registrations are pulled out with the exact sentence and where it came from.

Open questions

Anything uncertain becomes a question for a person, such as "Does the restriction exclude this agency?" It is not a legal determination.

03Effort

Time estimates from the notice itself.

Drivers, each quoted

Letter of intent +2 · narrative page limit +1 to +3 · budget justification +1 · cost-share documentation +2 · letters of support +1 each (max 3) · evaluation or data plan +2 · registrations +1 · multi-stage review +2.

Bands

0–3 points = Low (about 1–2 weeks) · 4–7 = Medium (3–5 weeks) · 8+ = High (6+ weeks). No drivers found = Unknown — check notice, never Low.

Feasibility

Compared with days left and your prep time: Feasible, Tight, or Not enough time. Always labeled "Estimate — based on notice requirements".

04AI, checked

AI explains. The code checks it.

Structured output

The model returns JSON: matched objective, plan citation, a quote from the notice, reasons and confidence.

Verified against sources

The objective must exist in the profile and the quote must appear word for word in the notice. Failures are replaced and marked Needs a second look.

Safe by default

Retrieved text is treated as untrusted data, never as instructions. If AI is unavailable, rules and similarity take over and everything still works.

05Privacy filter

Everything you type is screened first.

The filter runs on the server, so it can't be bypassed from the browser. It screens every typed input: the survey's other priority, project search, decision reasons, decision notes, your initials and notes added to a brief. It runs before any AI call, any save, and any brief copy or download.

You always see what was removed. Text that looks internal is blocked until you edit it. Original text is never stored; only the count and type of redactions are logged.

Layer 1 · optional

Azure AI Language PII detection

Names, contact details and IDs, detected inside the Azure tenant when configured.

Layer 2 · always on

Local pattern filter

Emails, phone numbers, SSNs, card numbers (Luhn-checked), street addresses and names after titles or cue words become [EMAIL], [PHONE], [NAME]…

Layer 3 · always on

Internal-information block

"Confidential", "internal only", "draft budget", "personnel", "do not distribute" and shortfall amounts stop the action until you edit the text.
